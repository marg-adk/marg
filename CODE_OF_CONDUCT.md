# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

**Expected:** be kind and direct; assume good intent; give and accept constructive review;
focus on what is best for the project and its users; respect differing viewpoints.

**Unacceptable:** harassment, insults or derogatory comments; publishing others' private
information; trolling; sustained disruption; sexualised language or imagery.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not align with this code, and
may ban temporarily or permanently any contributor for behaviours they deem inappropriate.

Report a concern privately to the maintainers (see the repository's contact in SECURITY.md).
All reports are handled confidentially.

Adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
